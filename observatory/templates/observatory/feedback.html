{% icon "chevron" %} Privacy information — who is collecting this, and what happens to it

Who is collecting this

{{ controller }}, acting as data controller. Contact {{ contact_email }}.

Why

To understand public views on air quality monitoring and dashboard data, and to shape future monitoring and reporting.

Legal basis

Consent, under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. Taking part is voluntary.

What we collect

Only the answers below. Anonymous by default: no name, address or date of birth. Location is county-level only. Email is requested only if you opt in, and is stored separately from your answers.

What we don't collect

Health data, ethnicity, political opinions or other special category data.

Who sees it

The project team only. Findings are published in aggregated form; no individual response is identifiable. Anonymised aggregates may be shared with project partners and public authorities.

How long

{{ retention_period }}, then securely deleted. Contact emails are deleted once results are circulated.

Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability, and withdrawal of consent at any time without giving a reason. Note that once a response is fully anonymised and merged into the dataset it may no longer be identifiable for removal.

Complaints

Data Protection Commission (Ireland), www.dataprotection.ie — or the Information Commissioner's Office if you are based in Northern Ireland.
